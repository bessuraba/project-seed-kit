# Apify-specific launch/build playbook (checked 2026-09-03)

Source: Apify Academy's Actor Marketing Playbook.

**Validate the idea before building** (their own stated process):
- Check real search demand (Google/Trends volume) for the problem you're solving — near-zero
  volume means a real niche that needs direct outreach, not organic discovery.
- Growth direction matters more than current volume — a small-but-rising trend beats a
  large-but-flat one.
- Cheap community signal check: post in relevant Reddit/HN/Discord/X communities, see if anyone
  actually responds "I'd use that."
- Personal motivation is treated as a real filter, not fluff — genuine care predicts follow-through.

**Build sequence**: validate → pick tooling (universal scraper / template / raw SDK, based on site
complexity and flexibility needed) → build → test → publish & monetize.

**Monetization guidance**: research direct competitors' actual pricing and offering, and remember
the Actor bundles Apify's own platform value (scheduling, monitoring, proxies, integrations) as
part of what's being sold, not just the data.

**Concrete, documented launch channels** (their checklist, not generic advice):
- Product Hunt — launch Tuesday–Thursday morning, with demo video/screenshots.
- Hacker News "Show HN" / "How I Built This" — weekday 8–10am EST.
- Reddit — r/webdev, r/SideProject, r/automation, r/datascience — genuine participation, not drive-by posting.
- dev.to / Hashnode / Medium — technical build-story and "best X" roundup content.
- Short demo video/GIF, tagged so Apify's own social channels can pick it up.
